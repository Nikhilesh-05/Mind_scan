## Product Vision Template

**To develop an accessible, private, and highly accurate Multimodal Depression Detection System that analyzes text, audio, and visual cues simultaneously to provide objective mental health screening and encourage early clinical intervention.**

---

### 1. Audience
**Who are the users of the product?**
*   **Primary Audience:** Individuals seeking a private, low-barrier, and accessible baseline assessment of their mental well-being without the immediate real-world stigma, wait times, or high costs associated with early clinical visits.
*   **Secondary Audience:** Healthcare providers, therapists, and clinicians who can use the generated objective diagnostic reports as supplemental background data during clinical intake and patient evaluation.

### 2. Needs
**What problems does the product solve for the audience?**
*   **Primary Needs:** A reliable, unbiased, and private tool that accurately assesses symptoms of depression and encourages individuals to seek professional help early. It overcomes the subjective self-reporting biases and inconsistencies of traditional questionnaires like the PHQ-9.
*   **Secondary Needs:** A standardized, quantifiable, and multimodal baseline report that helps clinicians understand a patient's behavioral and physiological state (voice prosody, facial expressions) prior to a formal diagnosis, ultimately saving time and improving care during clinical intake.

### 3. Products
**What is the product, and what does it offer?**
*   **Core Product:** *MindScan* - A web-based AI system that acts as an automated preliminary mental health screening tool. It captures real-time conversational chat (Text), vocal tone (Audio), and facial expressions (Video) to compute a single integrated therapeutic "Fusion Score" (Low, Moderate, High risk) and delivers a standardized, actionable PDF report.
*   **Additional Features:** 
    * Real-time sentiment NLP classification via Sarvam AI.
    * Privacy-first edge computing architecture that processes facial emotion tracking directly in the user's browser without storing raw video/audio feeds.
    * Dynamic, interactive visual dashboards for score interpretation.

### 4. Values
**What principles guide the product, and what makes it unique?**
*   **Core Values:** Privacy, Accessibility, Objectivity, and Early Intervention. The system is designed to provide sophisticated screening techniques to anyone with a standard web browser while strictly adhering to data protection guidelines.
*   **Differentiators:** Multimodal fusion logic. Unlike simple text-based wellness chatbots or standard static PHQ-9 forms, MindScan observes a user holistically. By tracking language, voice tone anomalies, and subtle facial micro-expressions simultaneously, it mimics the complex holistic observation tactics of a trained human clinician.

### 5. Vision Statement
**What is the overarching goal of the product?**
To empower individuals with a universally accessible, privacy-first, and highly accurate AI-driven screening tool that bridges the crucial gap between digital self-assessment and clinical mental health support, fostering early intervention and holistic well-being.
