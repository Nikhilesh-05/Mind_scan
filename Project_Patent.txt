# Project Patent Disclosure: MindScan

**1. Title of invention**
MindScan: A Real-Time AI-Driven Multimodal Depression Detection and Risk Assessment System.

**2. Describe the invention.**
The invention is a combination of a software system and a diagnostic processing workflow. Its general purpose is to provide a highly accessible, objective, and automated preliminary screening for depressive symptoms. It operates by capturing and fusing three distinct streams of data—conversational text sentiment, vocal prosodic features (audio), and real-time facial micro-expressions (video)—into a unified, quantifiable "Fusion Score" that categorizes the user's mental health risk level (Low, Moderate, High) instantly without requiring a human clinician to be present.

**3. Does the invention provide a new use of or improvement to an existing product or process?**
Yes, it provides a significant improvement to existing psychiatric screening processes. Traditional screening relies almost entirely on self-reporting diagnostic questionnaires (e.g., PHQ-9 or BDI) and face-to-face clinician interviews. These existing processes suffer from subjective reporting bias (users downplaying their symptoms) and high resource costs (clinician time). This invention improves upon existing digital mental health applications—which typically only analyze text—by fusing passive audio and video biometrics, creating a far more resilient, multi-dimensional, and objective measurement tool.

**4. State the Novelty of the invention and specify the claims in the invention**
*Novelty:* The primary novelty lies in the simultaneous, real-time client-side synchronization and algorithmic weighting (Fusion Engine) of three separate modalities (Text, Audio, Video) within a lightweight web browser environment while strictly preserving data privacy (processing visual frames transiently without permanently saving raw video).
*Claims:* 
1. A method for calculating a synthesized depression risk metric by dynamically weighting text sentiment, vocal pitch/energy variants, and facial muscle movements captured via standard consumer hardware.
2. An algorithmic approach to applying localized scoring multipliers (e.g., boosting 'sad' facial micro-expressions while suppressing 'disgust' false-positives) tailored specifically for mental health diagnostics rather than general emotion recognition.
3. A system pipeline that immediately converts the multimodal findings into an interpretable, secure PDF clinical report.

**5. Describe the advantages of the present invention over the existing technologies**
- **Efficiency:** Produces an actionable risk assessment in under 5 minutes, compared to days or weeks waiting for a psychiatric evaluation.
- **Cost Benefits:** Drastically lowers the barrier to entry for preliminary screening; requires only a smartphone or laptop with a camera and microphone.
- **Objectivity & Accuracy:** Counters the subjective bias of self-reporting by passively measuring physiological outputs (voice pitch, micro-expressions).
- **Privacy vs Stigma:** Allows users to conduct an initial screening in the privacy of their own home, avoiding the stigma often associated with visiting a mental health clinic.

**6. Describe how the present invention overcomes the drawbacks of currently available technology related to your invention.**
Currently available digital mental health tools primarily utilize unimodal text analysis (chatbots). The drawback of unimodal systems is that a user can type "I am fine," successfully deceiving the text analyzer, while their facial affect remains flat and their voice lacks energy. The present multimodal invention overcomes this by cross-referencing modalities. If text sentiment contradicts the audio/video vectors, the Fusion Engine adjusts the risk score accordingly, resulting in a significantly more resilient detection boundary.

**7. Describe uses, applications and benefits of the invention.**
- **Uses:** Preliminary remote screening for depression, continuous mental wellness tracking for individuals, and triaging patient priority in overloaded psychiatric clinics.
- **Applications:** Telehealth platforms, corporate employee wellness programs, university student counseling services, and integration into existing EMR (Electronic Medical Record) systems.
- **Benefits:** Facilitates early intervention, reduces the workload on human clinicians by filtering out low-risk cases, and provides patients with immediate, personalized self-care recommendations and objective data.

**8. Does the focus of the invention results in societal impact technology?**
Yes, deeply so. The World Health Organization estimates that 5% of adults globally suffer from depression, yet the majority do not receive care due to systemic resource bottlenecks and societal stigma. This invention directly addresses the market need for scalable, affordable, and private mental health care. Commercially, telehealth providers and health insurance companies have a massive financial incentive to catch depressive symptoms early before they require expensive hospitalization. The product serves this exact market, democratizing access to clinical-grade screening. 

**9. Characterize the disadvantages and limitations of the invention**
- **Hardware Dependency:** It requires a relatively modern device with a functioning microphone, webcam, and stable internet connection.
- **Environmental Variables:** Audio recording quality can be heavily degraded by background noise, and video tracking accuracy drops significantly in low lighting conditions or when the face is partially obscured.
- **Diagnostic Authority:** The system is strictly a risk-assessment and screening tool; it cannot legally replace a licensed psychiatrist for a formal medical diagnosis or prescription.

**11. Current development status of the invention**

**A. Has your invention been tested experimentally?**
Yes, early-stage experimental testing has been conducted in a controlled environment.

**B. Describe the experimental approach of the invention also state the methods adopted in the experiment.**
The experimental approach involved running standardized usability and accuracy tests on the completed web platform. Specifically, users were asked to engage the text chat by simulating varying levels of depressive language. Concurrently, the video module's accuracy was tested by subjects exhibiting constrained facial expressions (simulated flat affect and sadness for 30 seconds). A specific method adopted was adjusting the algorithmic thresholds (e.g., applying a 2.5x sensitivity multiplier to the "sad" emotion output array) to determine the threshold required to minimize false positives against "neutral" expressions.

**C. Are the experimental data documented in a formal log or any instrumental confirmation available for the invention (kindly provide the details)**
The raw interaction data is automatically documented in the system's PostgreSQL database under the `chat_results`, `audio_results`, and `video_results` tables. Furthermore, each test session generates an instrumental confirmation in the form of a dynamically rendered `Evaluation Report (PDF)`, detailing the exact confidence intervals, mathematical Fusion Score, and the granular breakdown of risk per modality.

**D. Is further development of your invention necessary or development of the invention is in progress (provide the relevant information)**
Yes, development is actively in progress. Currently, the text and video modules process live data, while the audio sentiment module utilizes mock pipeline simulations. Further development requires replacing the mock audio endpoints with a deployed instance of OpenAI Whisper (for transcriptions) and Librosa (for live pitch/energy vector analysis). Additionally, the system requires formal clinical validation trials against traditional PHQ-9 cohorts to statistically verify its sensitivity and specificity.
